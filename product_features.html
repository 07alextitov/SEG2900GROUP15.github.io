PassPal is a downloadable desktop app that gives you full command over your credentials — no browser restrictions, no cloud dependencies.

Install PassPal directly on your computer. No browser extensions, no third-party sync — just pure local control.

🔐 Encrypted Vault

All passwords are stored using AES-256 encryption. Your vault is locked tight — only you hold the key.

📁 Cross-App Integration

Use PassPal across browsers, apps, and terminals. It’s not tied to Chrome, Firefox, or Edge — it’s tied to your machine.

Autofill credentials in any app or browser with keyboard shortcuts and context-aware detection.

Create separate vaults for work, personal, or shared use. Switch profiles instantly with secure authentication.

Track access history, changes, and login attempts. Stay informed and in control.
